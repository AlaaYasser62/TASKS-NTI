HORSE ADVENTURES

discover our favorite horses and the beautiful places they call home

this is a Friesian horse

this is a mountain horse
it is patient and sure-footed
it can walk through long mountain trails
and enjoy the fresh open air

this is a chestnut horse
it loves running beside the sea
the beach gives it plenty of open space
and a beautiful sunset view

these are wild horses
they live together on the open prairie
they run freely under the wide sky
and travel across the grasslands
